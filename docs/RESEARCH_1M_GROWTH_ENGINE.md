## 🧠 3. How Google's Helpful Content System (HCS) Evaluates Game Sites

Google's HCS algorithm penalizes simple iframe aggregators because they provide zero unique value. Poki and CrazyGames stay at #1 because they fulfill 4 strict criteria:

1. **Unique Text Volume**: Every page must have 800+ words of unique narrative, strategy guides, controls tables, and technical specs.
2. **Structured JSON-LD Schemas**: Pages must output `SoftwareApplication` / `VideoGame`, `FAQPage`, and `BreadcrumbList` schemas.
3. **Core Web Vitals & TTFB**: Instant initial HTML load (<100ms) with lazy-loaded game iframes so visitors don't bounce.
4. **Internal PageRank Mesh**: Dynamic internal linking connecting related games, categories, and tag hubs.
